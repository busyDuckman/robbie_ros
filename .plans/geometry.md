Neck linkage:
  - The rods are M3 threaded rod
  - They terminate into a 20mm push rod linkage (ball joint end, typical hobbyshop type)
  - end to end the rod assembly is 95mm
  - pivot to pivot spacing is about 87mm (give or take)
  - the 35mm (free length), 8mm od, extensions springs are mounted 30mm from the top pivot

Pivot locations:
  - the linkages join, lets use r = 43mm form the center axis (sas per scad file).
      - to old unit measured a tad under 50mm (we ill soon discover if thats important or not)
  - the linkages join r = 40mm from the top platform center

Misc:
    - platform rest height is about 70mm (very rough, the old robbie is partly disassembled atm).

Notes:
  - springs are small and spec is not important, just that they resist pulling, will give basically the same behaviour.
  - mass on the plate is also not that relevant, so long as its not a brick it should not back drive the motors.
  - I'm not sure what the limits on the ball joints are, that seems to determine the final resting point
